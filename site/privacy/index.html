Privacy

Foldertrail reads your BaseCamp library inside your browser, on your computer. Your library is never uploaded.

  • Uploaded

    Nothing. Not your library, not the files Foldertrail makes.

  • Sent to another site

    Only for the license: your license key and Foldertrail’s product ID, to the payment provider, when you unlock the download, and to re-check a saved key at most once per browser session.

  • Kept in your browser

    A license key the payment provider accepted, so you don’t have to paste it again.

  • Analytics, cookies, ads

    None.

What Foldertrail reads

Only the files you give it: your library folder, a BaseCamp backup file, or a library package. It reads them in the browser tab, on your computer, and makes your download there too.

The waypoints, tracks, routes, names and notes in your library are never sent anywhere. Foldertrail only reads your library; it never changes it.

What is sent, and to whom

One thing: your license key and Foldertrail’s public product ID, sent to the payment provider (Polar or Gumroad) so it can say whether the key is valid. That happens when you unlock the download, and again at most once per browser session when you open the converter with a key saved in your browser, so that a refunded key stops working. Nothing from your library goes with it.

The check sends no cookies. Like any request on the internet, it also carries your IP address and the address of this site. The payment provider’s own privacy policy covers that check, and the checkout page if you buy: buying happens on the provider’s site, not this one.

What is stored in your browser

If the payment provider accepts your key, Foldertrail keeps the key in your browser’s local storage, so you don’t have to paste it again. For the current browser session only, it also notes that the key was already checked. Nothing else is stored: not your library, not your email address.

To remove the key, clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings, or choose Remove it where the converter says a license key is saved. Foldertrail sets no cookies.

What the web host sees

A hosting company serves these pages. Like any website, its servers see ordinary request information, such as your IP address and which pages and files your browser loaded. Foldertrail adds no analytics, tracking or ads on top of that, and loads nothing from other sites.

The diagnostic report

If something looks wrong, the converter offers a diagnostic report. It describes only the structure of the library files: sizes, counts, lengths and version numbers. It holds no names, notes, places, coordinates, times or file paths.

Foldertrail never sends it. You can copy it, read it, and decide whether to share it.

An extra safety layer

Every page is served with a strict Content Security Policy. It tells your browser not to load code from other sites, and not to connect to any site except the payment providers’ license checks.

It is a second layer behind the code itself, not a promise you can check from the page. What keeps your library on your computer is that Foldertrail’s code never sends it.
