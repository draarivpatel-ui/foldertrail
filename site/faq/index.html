Questions and answers

Short answers. The guide has the step-by-step version.

Before you start

Do I need BaseCamp installed?

No. Foldertrail reads the library files themselves, so it works after BaseCamp stops opening. If BaseCamp still opens, a backup file (File › Backup) is the simplest thing to drop in.

Which libraries can it read?

The library folder BaseCamp for Mac keeps (a folder named like 4.8), a BaseCamp backup file, or a .bclibrary package. The guide shows where to find your library.

If your library is in a format Foldertrail can’t read, the free preview tells you.

Which browsers work?

Current versions of Safari, Chrome and Firefox on a Mac.

Does it work with BaseCamp for Windows, or on a phone?

It’s made for libraries from BaseCamp for Mac. Libraries from BaseCamp for Windows may work, but they haven’t been checked yet. On a phone it can’t help, because your library is on your computer.

I have an Intel Mac, or I’m staying on macOS 27. Do I need this?

Not urgently: BaseCamp keeps running there. Foldertrail is still useful if you want your library in other apps with its folders kept.

Your files

Is my library uploaded?

No. The page reads your library inside your browser, on your computer, and makes your download there too. The privacy page has the details.

What does the license check send?

Your license key and Foldertrail’s product ID, to the payment provider (Polar or Gumroad), when you unlock the download. If your key is saved in the browser, it is checked again at most once per browser session, when you open the converter. Nothing from your library goes with it.

Does it change my library?

No. Foldertrail only reads it. Keeping a safety copy is still a good idea: the guide shows how to make one.

The results

Will my folders show up in Garmin Explore, Gaia GPS or CalTopo?

GPX files can’t hold folders, so your folders come out as folders of files on your Mac. Each app then shows them its own way. Gaia GPS makes one folder per imported file. Garmin’s help for Explore doesn’t describe collections inside collections. CalTopo sorts imports into its own folders. Google Earth shows the whole tree from the KML file. The guide has the steps for each app.

Why are my routes straight lines?

Foldertrail writes each route as straight lines through its via points; a GPS device can work out the roads again when you navigate it. Routes that BaseCamp calculated along roads aren’t converted in this version: the preview lists them under “What will not convert”, and the report names them.

An item is in several lists. Will I get copies?

Each list’s file includes it, so importing several lists gives you several copies. To avoid that, import one list at a time, or All items.gpx once.

Some items aren’t where I expected. Why?

BaseCamp’s lists point to items by name. When two items of the same kind share a name, Foldertrail can’t tell which one a list means, so it doesn’t guess: every copy goes into a _Duplicate names folder, and the report names the lists that refer to them. Items that are in no list go into Not in any list.gpx.

What doesn’t convert?

BirdsEye imagery, custom maps, Adventures, geocache logs and photos. When Foldertrail finds some of these in your library, such as BirdsEye imagery, the preview and the report mention them. The report also lists anything else that couldn’t be read.

Price

What does it cost?

The preview is free. Downloading the files will need a license key; the planned price is $19.

Not on sale yet: Foldertrail isn’t on sale, so downloading is switched off for now. The preview is complete and free in the meantime.

What if my library doesn’t convert cleanly?

Foldertrail has been checked against real libraries made in BaseCamp 4.8.13 for Mac (with made-up content), but yours may hold something we haven’t seen yet. That is why the preview is free: it shows your tree and what it found that won’t convert, so you can check it against your library (in BaseCamp, if it still opens) before you pay.

Help

Is Foldertrail made by Garmin?

No. It’s an independent tool, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Garmin.

Something looks wrong. How do I get help?

Use the diagnostic report on the converter page. It describes only the structure of your library files, with no names, places or file paths, so you can share it safely. A support address will be listed here when Foldertrail goes on sale.
